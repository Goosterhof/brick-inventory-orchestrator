_The Quality Warden's private notebook. Suspicions, patterns, and unresolved threads that carry across inspections. The Pulse tracks what The Steward acknowledges. The Casebook tracks what the Quality Warden notices._

_Read this before every inspection. Write to it after every inspection. Suspicions that prove unfounded get crossed out with a reason — they don't get deleted. The Quality Warden's memory includes what it got wrong._
